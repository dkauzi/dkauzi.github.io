Starlace – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Starlace ("the game") is published by kauzi. This page explains what information is handled when you play.

What the game itself stores

The game has no accounts and asks for no personal details. Your best scores, stardust, unlocked ships and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us.

Advertising

The game shows ads between rounds using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which indicates approximate location), device and app information, how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data. Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice from the consent message, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings under Privacy → Ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data we hold

We do not operate servers for this game and hold no personal data about players. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
